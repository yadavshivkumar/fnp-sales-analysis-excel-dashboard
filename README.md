# fnp-sales-analysis-excel-dashboard

An interactive sales analysis dashboard built in Excel evaluating gift orders, revenue by occasion, category performance, customer spending, and delivery time metrics for Ferns and Petals (FNP).



# 🎁 Ferns and Petals (FNP) Sales Analysis Dashboard


## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes gift sales data for **Ferns and Petals (FNP)**, a major gifting brand specializing in occasions like Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries[cite: 15, 16]. The objective is to uncover actionable insights regarding sales trends, customer behavior, delivery efficiency, and product performance[cite: 15].

## 🔍 Core Business Questions Addressed

1. **Revenue by Occasions:** Evaluates revenue generated across different festivals and milestones (*Diwali, Birthday, Anniversary, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day*)[cite: 15, 16].

2. **Category & Product Performance:** Analyzes revenue distribution across categories (Cakes, Mugs, Plants, Sweets, Soft Toys, etc.) and tracks top-performing revenue-generating products[cite: 15, 16].

3. **Monthly Sales Fluctuations:** Tracks seasonal sales trends across the months of 2023[cite: 15].

4. **Geographic Distribution:** Identifies the top 10 cities placing the highest volume of orders[cite: 15, 16].

5. **Operational Efficiency:** Analyzes order delivery times and investigates the correlation between order quantities and shipping durations[cite: 15].
